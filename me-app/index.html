Ask your documents

Answered from the documents your appliance has ingested — shared folders you ticked to index, uploads, pages you saved. Every claim carries a citation you can open: local files reveal in Finder, web sources open where they came from.

Dates are the document's own — when the file was last changed or created — not when your appliance happened to index it. A document whose source never carried that date is excluded by a date filter rather than guessed at.

Local files

Share folders — Documents, a projects directory — with your appliance. Each appears read-only inside the assistant container under /local, and Apply wires it into the assistant's virtual Cypher: "what changed this week?", "which files mention the renewal?" are answered by walking your folders live — nothing is copied or stored. Tick index contents to go further for a folder: its documents (PDFs included) are embedded into the knowledge base, so they answer above alongside anything you dropped in. Applying rewrites docker-compose.override.yml and restarts the assistant container; the graph and everything it remembers stay up.

Query Studio — virtual Cypher

The advanced surface, in its own window so this one stays simple. The studio composes real virtual-Cypher from the engine's primitives — three relevance modes (about, mentions, and judged against an intent brief), tag and date scopes, {ai:{…}} steering — over documents, local files, and email threads. Everything it composes is editable before it runs; your queries only ever see your own data, a scope the appliance enforces, not the query.

Handler Studio — TypeScript that reacts

The sibling surface for smallish handlers: TypeScript against the typed gateway.* surface, reacting to a signal or a cron tick. Written by hand or from English, type-checked by the appliance's own compiler gate, and always dry-runnable — observe-only, against a real recent signal — before anything is enabled to act.

Apps

Models

Which model does what. Models running on this Mac — LM Studio, Ollama — cost nothing to run and keep the work on your machine; hosted ones are billed to your own provider key. A change takes effect on the next piece of work: no restart. Starting a NEW local model is the exception — the appliance registers models at boot, so restart it to pick one up.
