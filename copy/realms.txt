You extend a world by installing a realm: a folder of declarative YAML in git.
Drop one in and the runtime wires up what it declares — types, actions, goals,
APIs, MCP servers, scheduled work, saved queries, whole dashboards. Nothing to
compile, and nothing you cannot read before you trust it.

If you are writing one, the appliance can read it straight off this machine,
with nothing published anywhere first. Give the directory your realms live IN,
so that adding another is a copy rather than a change here.

You do not have to write the YAML by hand. The appliance is an MCP server, so
your coding agent can reach the live world — its types, its data, what is
already installed — and author saved views, handlers and whole realms against
it, then test them against real answers rather than guesses.
