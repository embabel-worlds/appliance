Extend your world by installing realms.
Realms extend your knowledge graph and add agents.
Typically, they bring in external systems, data sources and applications.
You can install realms from our realm store or author them yourself.

If you are writing your own, they live in a directory on this machine, which
your world reads live so an edit shows up without reinstalling anything. One is
already set up for you — the directory named below — so there is nothing to do
here until you have a realm to put in it.

To keep them somewhere else, say so whenever you like:

    embabel realms link ~/dev

That wants the directory your realms sit IN, not a realm itself.
