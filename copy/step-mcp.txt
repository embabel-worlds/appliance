This appliance serves two MCP endpoints, and one token opens both:

    /mcp/chat    a chat client — the assistant's own tools, over your world's data
    /mcp/code    a coding agent — realms, authoring, mining a system into one

Plain /mcp is the chat door as well, and is what most clients fill in for you.

The token stays on this machine and becomes active when setup completes and the
appliance restarts.
