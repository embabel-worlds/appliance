Documents are searched by meaning, not by keyword, which needs an embedding
model. The appliance does not ship with one: the local model is about a
gigabyte and needs Docker Model Runner, and an install should not spend that
before you have decided you want it.

Everything else works without it — the graph, realms, views, handlers, chat.
Only uploading and searching documents wait on this, and they will say so
rather than failing quietly.

You can turn it on at any time with `embabel embeddings use local`.
